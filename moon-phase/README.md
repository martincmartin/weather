# Moon phase images

`phase-00.png` to `phase-29.png` are the moon at 0/30 to 29/30 of a lunation,
for `/stargazing.bmp`. They are renders by NASA's Scientific Visualization
Studio (Dial-A-Moon, <https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/gallery/moonphase/>), from Lunar
Reconnaissance Orbiter data. NASA material is not under copyright. Credit:
NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.

`make_moon_phases.py` downloads and crops them; its docstring gives the details.
